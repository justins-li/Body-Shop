{# Body outline used by the weekly summary. Usage: {% import "partials/_body_figure.html" as body %} {{ body.figure("front") }} Each trainable region is an SVG path with `class="muscle"` and a `data-muscle` slug. `app/static/js/summary.js` sets a `--level` custom property (0–1) on every region and toggles `.is-worked` / `.is-over`, which shades it along the green or red volume ramp (see `.muscle.is-worked` and `.muscle.is-over` in css/styles.css). The two views share no muscle groups: Front — chest (two pectorals), abs (upper + lower), biceps, quads. Back — back (traps + lats), triceps, hamstrings. `.body-base` draws a complete silhouette (head, torso, arms, legs, extremities) underneath, so muscle regions are overlays and can leave anatomical gaps — sternum, ribs, obliques, glutes, lower back — without holes in the figure. A group may be drawn as several paths; `summary.js` selects on `data-muscle`, so they light up together. #} {% macro figure(view) %} {% endmacro %}
