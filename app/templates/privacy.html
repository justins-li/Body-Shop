Privacy

Body Shop is a workout log. It holds the sets you write down and the email address you signed up with, and it is built so that there is very little else to hold.

What is stored

Your email address, and every set you log — the movement, the date, the weight in kilograms, the reps, and the RPE if you record one.

Anything the app stores about you is something you typed into it. Nothing is collected in the background, and if that list ever grows it grows here too.

What is not stored

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, and no third-party scripts on any page. Your password is held by Supabase and never reaches this app.

Nobody is compared to anybody. The estimated one-rep max on the progress page is worked out from your own sets; the app holds no strength standards and no population data to rank you against.

Who else sees it

Supabase holds your email address, your password and the database your workouts live in. Render runs the app, so its logs record the requests your browser makes, including your IP address. Sentry receives a report when something breaks; it is configured not to send personal data with one.

jsDelivr serves the exercise photographs. Your browser fetches those images from their servers directly, which means they see your IP address when it does. The images can be moved to our own origin by configuration, and this paragraph will change when they are.

Your data is yours

Download everything you have logged as a CSV file, any time, from your account. It is the whole log, not a summary of it.

Delete your account from the same page. That removes the account and every workout in it, including the sign-in record held by Supabase. It cannot be undone, and nothing is kept back.
